Product Care

Practical care notes for paper goods and desk essentials.

PAPER

Keep it dry

Store notebooks and paper goods away from moisture, direct heat and prolonged sunlight.

WRITING

Protect the tips

Keep fine-liners and mechanical pencils capped or stored securely when not in use.

DESK

Keep surfaces clean

Dust organizers and trays regularly with a soft, dry cloth.
